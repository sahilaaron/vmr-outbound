Uploading imports nothing. You will see exactly what would happen — every row, every match and every conflict — and nothing is written until you confirm.

What this expects, and what it does

The file

An Apollo contact export. Columns are recognised by their header names, in any order, and extra columns are fine — nothing has to be mapped by hand. The header row must name First Name, Last Name, Company Name and Email.

The address

The Email column is used as supplied. No candidate addresses are generated and no verification provider is called — not MillionVerifier, not any other. Whatever the export says about an address (its status, its verification source, its last-verified date) is kept and shown as the vendor's claim. It is not a VMR verification and does not mean the mailbox exists.

The company

Company Name is not trusted on its own — Apollo exports routinely carry a name that disagrees with the website, the email domain and the LinkedIn page. When those agree, that is the company; when they disagree, the row is held for you rather than filed under a guess.

Who can see this

This application is single-operator today: there are no user accounts, so an import is scoped to a campaign and not to a person. An upload can only be confirmed into the campaign it was uploaded for, and one campaign's import cannot be opened from another.
