Rules the panel follows

A person is captured whether or not a campaign is selected. The campaign is filing, not permission.
Capturing someone twice does not create a second person — the second capture updates the first.
The panel only reads pages you have open. It never navigates, scrolls or clicks on your behalf.
The original snapshot is stored verbatim and never edited, so any derived value can be checked against what was on the page.
Someone who cannot be resolved is still captured permanently. They wait for a decision rather than being discarded.
