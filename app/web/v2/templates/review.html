Sequences

Each card is one contact's seven-message sequence, written as one unit and approved as written. Open a card to read the messages one at a time. Reviewing is optional — nothing is held up waiting for you — and confirming a message records your judgement against that exact version so it can be told apart from the default. Nothing is sent either way: there is no sending path and no mailbox connected to this build.
