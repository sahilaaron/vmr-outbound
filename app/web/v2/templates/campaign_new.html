The three later steps

Audience rules{{ unbuilt() }}
Role, industry, geography and size criteria that decide who a capture may join. There is no rules engine yet — you enrol contacts from the Contacts screen instead, which is explicit and reversible.
Message shape{{ unbuilt() }}
The ask, the tone and the length cap. Today the Personalization Agent works from your Knowledge Base guardrails, which are set once and apply everywhere.
Sending{{ unbuilt() }}
Mailboxes, a daily cap, a send window and an auto-send threshold. None exists: there is no sending adapter, so every draft waits for your approval and goes no further.
Showing these as steps rather than hiding them is deliberate. You should be able to see what the product will ask for, without a form implying it will act on the answer.
