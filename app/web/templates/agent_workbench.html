What the controls can and cannot do

Agent controls decide what executes. They never decide what is permitted. Suppression, Campaign Contact eligibility, exact-draft approval and verification truth stay authoritative above every control on these pages: enabling an Agent cannot release a blocked Contact, and a retry cannot bypass a suppression, a disabled Agent, or a Campaign override.

No control discards work. Pausing and disabling hold claimable and leased jobs at paused; a retry requeues the same durable job under its existing identity, so completed work cannot be duplicated. A stage is shown as complete only when Phase 2 committed a domain outcome and a pipeline event to say so — a succeeded job alone is never enough.
