Intelligence vocabulary

A vocabulary is a released edition, not a list somebody edits. Publishing a corrected list creates a new version and retires the old one; classifications made under the old edition keep resolving, and nothing already stored is rewritten.

Dimensions not listed here — products, services, specialties, capabilities and specific geographies — have no controlled vocabulary in this release. They record the producer's own wording, which is honest rather than a gap: a partial list of them would reject correct values while looking complete.
