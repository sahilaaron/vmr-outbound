Boundary

Agent Studio adds inspection and Agent-specific modules. It does not create another execution-control service. Status changes remain on the existing Workbench routes and preserve stored configuration, including {"live": true}.

Suppression, evidence eligibility, Campaign execution, Campaign overrides, exact-draft approval and Sending availability remain separate authorities.
